About Laura

Laura Pirie's work moves between playful observation and bold emotion, bringing animals, flowers, figures, and everyday objects into saturated, painterly focus.

Expressive Color

Layered palettes and loose marks give each piece a lively, immediate presence.

Character Studies

Portraits and creatures are painted with personality, humor, and an eye for mood.

Nature & Still Life

Botanical subjects and familiar forms become bright, tactile moments on canvas.
